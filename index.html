Our Approach

Coaching Tailored To The Individual.

  • Designed by ex-professional players
  • Safe, supportive environment
  • Creativity over conformity
  • Long-term player development

Every player is different. At Ekor FC, we coach the child in front of us, not a template.

Our coaching syllabus was built by ex-professional soccer players who learned the game at the highest level and want to pass it on the right way. We focus on each child as an individual, meeting them where they are, and helping them develop their own way of playing.

Every session, every field, every age group shares the same principles: a safe, structured environment where kids feel confident enough to be creative, take risks, and fall in love with the game. There's no rush. We develop players over the long term, not just for the next game.

Our Story

From the Pitch to the Community.

Ekor FC was founded by Edwin and Kevin Orduy, brothers, former professional soccer players, and lifelong students of the game. Their careers took them across the Americas and Europe: Rio Grande Valley FC, Laredo Heat, Unión Magdalena in Colombia, and clubs in Brazil and Spain.

Growing up in Colombia, soccer wasn't just a sport. It was how the neighborhood gathered. It was family on the weekends, friends after school, and the unwritten rule that everyone got to play. That feeling is what they're building here in Orlando.

Ekor is a place where kids learn from coaches who've been there, where teams and families can come together to play, and where the love of the game gets passed down the way it's always been passed down, by playing, together.
